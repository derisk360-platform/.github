# Security Policy

## Reporting a Vulnerability

Report security issues to support@derisk360.com. Do not open public issues for vulnerabilities.

Include:

- Affected repository and branch
- Reproduction steps
- Potential impact
- Relevant logs or screenshots with secrets redacted

## Response Targets

- Critical: triage within 1 business day
- High: triage within 2 business days
- Medium: triage within 5 business days
- Low: triage within 10 business days

## Required Security Controls

- Mandatory 2FA for organization members and outside collaborators
- Branch protection on `main`
- CODEOWNERS review for sensitive paths
- Dependabot alerts and security updates
- Secret scanning and push protection where available
- Least-privilege team permissions
